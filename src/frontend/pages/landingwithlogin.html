Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ReTrade

How does the escrow payment work?

When you buy an item, payment is held securely in escrow. The seller only receives funds after delivery is confirmed.

What happens if I win an auction?

You'll receive an instant notification and have 24 hours to complete payment.

How is the seller trust score calculated?

Trust scores are based on successful deliveries, ratings, cancellation rates, response time and verification status.

Can I sell internationally?

Currently we support domestic sales with international shipping planned for the future.
